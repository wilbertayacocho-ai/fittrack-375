Progression rule

Keep most sets at about 2–3 reps in reserve (RIR). First add reps; once every set reaches the top of its range with clean form for 2 sessions, increase band tension slightly. Don't chase failure on every set.

With hypertension, breathe continuously and avoid breath-holding or maximal straining. Stop if you feel chest pain, unusual breathlessness, dizziness, or a severe headache.

Exercise how-to library

Use the exercise photos and movement cues as a quick reference. Start light, move slowly, and stop if you feel pain. For band exercises, inspect bands and anchors before each set.

Band row

  1. Anchor band securely at chest height and step back until taut.
  2. Keep torso tall; pull elbows toward your ribs.
  3. Pause briefly, then return slowly without rounding your back.
Cue: elbows back; shoulders down.

Band biceps curl

  1. Stand on the band with feet hip-width apart.
  2. Keep elbows close to your sides and curl hands toward shoulders.
  3. Lower under control; avoid swinging your body.
Cue: elbows stay still; wrists neutral.

Band triceps extension

  1. Secure band overhead or behind you; hold elbows close to your head/body.
  2. Straighten elbows to move hands away from shoulders.
  3. Return slowly without letting elbows flare widely.
Cue: upper arms steady; don't arch your back.

Band chest press

  1. Place band behind upper back and hold handles at chest height.
  2. Brace gently and press hands forward without locking elbows hard.
  3. Bring hands back slowly; keep ribs down.
Cue: don't shrug or hold your breath.

Band squat

  1. Stand on band, feet about shoulder-width, hold ends near shoulders.
  2. Send hips back and bend knees, keeping whole foot grounded.
  3. Stand by pushing the floor away; keep knees tracking toes.
Cue: chest comfortable, knees follow toes.

Band Romanian deadlift

  1. Stand on band with knees softly bent and hands in front of thighs.
  2. Push hips backward, sliding hands down thighs; keep back neutral.
  3. Drive hips forward to stand tall; don't lean backward at the top.
Cue: move from hips, not from the low back.

Push-up

  1. Hands slightly wider than shoulders; make a straight line from head to heels.
  2. Lower chest between hands with elbows angled slightly back.
  3. Push floor away while keeping hips from sagging.
Cue: body straight; exhale as you push.

Pull-up

  1. Use a secure bar; start from a controlled hang with shoulders engaged.
  2. Pull elbows down and bring chest toward the bar without kicking.
  3. Lower slowly to the start. Use assistance if needed.
Cue: no swinging; stop before form breaks.

Dips

  1. Grip stable parallel bars and support yourself with elbows straight but not forcefully locked.
  2. Lower only as far as shoulders feel comfortable.
  3. Press up smoothly; keep shoulders controlled.
Cue: pain-free range only; avoid deep dips if shoulders hurt.

Sit-up

  1. Lie on your back with knees bent and feet supported lightly.
  2. Exhale and curl torso up without yanking your neck.
  3. Lower slowly; stop if your back or neck hurts.
Cue: use your abs, not a neck pull.

Plank

  1. Place forearms under shoulders and extend legs behind you.
  2. Keep head, hips, and heels in one line; brace gently.
  3. Breathe normally and stop before hips sag or rise.
Cue: breathe normally; no breath-holding.

Muscle-up practice

Advanced skill: practice only if you already have controlled pull-ups and dips. Do not attempt fatigued or without a secure bar and clear space.
  1. Start with a strong, controlled pull and bring chest close to the bar.
  2. Transition wrists and shoulders over the bar only with established technique.
  3. Press to support, then descend under control. Stop if technique gets messy.
Cue: low-volume skill practice, never grind reps.

Plank / dead bug core

  1. For dead bug: lie on back, arms up and knees over hips.
  2. Slowly extend opposite arm and leg while keeping lower back gently down.
  3. Return and alternate sides; exhale during each extension.
Cue: slow control beats speed.
